## What the traces support, and what they do not

The exact original smoothness definition was independently recomputed from 520 complete state CSVs (five primary methods × 104 scenarios), matching the summary values. The traces directly show nonzero secondary task leakage and larger command changes at 48 Hz posture refreshes than during the four held physical steps. Mean episode-average `||J N_lambda u||` is 0.08596/0.14499/0.15641/0.11153 mm/s for APF/550901/551901/552901. Neither global speed scaling nor soft-limit clipping is activated in this common-success population. Thus the reported roughness here is not explained by those two execution limiters.

Across the three policies, higher mean raw clipping coexists with larger mean posture magnitude and rougher commands for the first two seeds. This is an observed tradeoff, not a causal result. In fact, the descriptive within-policy scene-level Pearson correlations between component clipping and smoothness are **−0.432, −0.518, and −0.288** on the 104 common successes. These dependent-scene associations rule out a simple statement that more clipping necessarily causes more roughness in every scene.

For seed 551901, the roughest common-success scene is `me5418-scenes-v1-0477` (near-link, template formal-g013). At 1.0625 s, the adjacent posture decisions differ by 0.17805 rad/s in 7D norm and the first resulting executed commands differ by 0.15018 rad/s. Its episode smoothness is 6.62154 rad/s², although only 14.29% of raw action components clip. This is a post hoc explanatory example, not a newly selected model or performance sample. Policy feedback sensitivity, changing closest-point geometry, and clipping are possible contributors; none was isolated by retraining or ablation.

The bounded reward's action and smoothness terms are each at most 0.02 reward/s in magnitude, or 0.08 over four seconds, while terminal outcomes are +20 and −20. This code-level objective scale gives a plausible explanation for prioritizing task completion over gentleness. It does not establish that changing one coefficient would improve performance. The actual policies remain frozen.

## Best/last retention is not monotonic

All last policies are evaluated as precommitted secondary models. Seed 550901 falls from 129 to 125 successes, with 0 gains and 4 losses; seed 551901 rises from 127 to 129, with 3 gains and 1 loss; seed 552901 falls from 131 to 125, with 0 gains and 6 losses. Longer training therefore does not uniformly improve or uniformly degrade held-out behavior. Validation-selected best retains its primary role even when last scores higher on the opened original test.

For seed 552901, the jointly complete best/last subset has n=125: mean command smoothness increases from 0.11337 to 0.41816 rad/s², maximum tracking error from 0.05077 to 0.09270 mm, and self clearance decreases from 19.7413 to 18.1550 mm. Four last-model failures are self-contact-band events. In `me5418-scenes-v1-0067`, the last policy first reaches the self-contact band at 3.875 s with self distance 0.07820 mm, below the +0.1 mm failure threshold; its endpoint error is null because it did not complete. Those partial prefixes cannot be ranked as smoother completed motions.

## Damped projection and self-safety limits

The shared controller uses `J_damped# = J^T (J J^T + lambda² I)^−1`, lambda=0.02 m/rad, and `N_lambda = I − J_damped# J`. Algebra gives `J N_lambda = lambda² (J J^T + lambda² I)^−1 J`, which is generally nonzero. The mapping is a damped approximate redundancy projection, not an exact position nullspace or a safety certificate. The trace records and tracking-error checks quantify the implemented behavior; replacing this controller would change the frozen APF/PPO contract and require a separately validated experiment.

The 91D observation contains external-link distances/normals/validity flags, but no explicit self-distance. Joint positions still provide indirect geometric information; absence of an explicit distance feature does not prove that self avoidance cannot be learned. The dense reward uses external sphere proximity, not self proximity or explicit joint-bound distance. Self collision and hard limits are still checked at every physical step, followed by termination and the failure terminal penalty. The best 551901 policy has one original self-contact-band failure and last 552901 has four. The terminal signal exists; an explicit self-geometry dense signal does not. Adding one is a future hypothesis, not an established cure.

## Dependence, screening, and the supplementary evaluation

The supplementary APF search completes the predeclared 3×3 grid: influence range 0.06/0.10/0.14 m and repulsion gain 2/4/8 rad²/(m·s), with the original joint-limit term unchanged. All nine candidates complete all 58 validation scenarios (522 episodes, zero failures and zero training interactions). The search budget is 1,800 s and measured execution is 424.689 s. Therefore the primary success criterion is saturated on validation. The predeclared ascending mean complete-success tracking-RMSE tie-break selects `APF_grid_00`, range 0.06 m and gain 2, with RMSE 0.042299 mm; the original fixed parameters are `APF_grid_04` with RMSE 0.047512 mm on the same validation scenarios. This is reproducible tuning and a validation motion-metric difference, not evidence that APF success has improved. The selected shorter-range/weaker repulsion remains frozen even if its supplementary success is worse. The grid includes the original fixed APF and records every result; it is a finite parameter search, not proof of global APF optimality. Exact values and nonmissing clearance n=22 are in `validation_grid_analysis.json`.

All per-category and per-template results, including failures, are provided in CSV/JSON. The declared sensitivity analysis resamples complete template groups with replacement (G groups per replicate, B=5,000, NumPy PCG64 seed 12042026), keeping paired methods on identical group draws. Scene-weighted differences use the number of scenarios retained in each draw; group sizes vary. The percentile intervals are descriptive post hoc sensitivity summaries, not significance tests or validated population confidence guarantees. Template groups themselves are not established independent population draws.

For original PPO best minus fixed APF, net differences and template-cluster 95% percentile ranges are respectively: +4.580 pp [−1.739, 14.286], +3.053 pp [−2.308, 10.072], and +6.107 pp [0.000, 16.000]. This is much weaker evidence than treating all variants or repeated seeds as independent samples. No model is selected from these intervals.

The original feasible set is conditioned on finite witness discovery: 330 of 340 accepted scenarios were discovered with the current-APF witness, six with a gentle APF, and four with predeclared u. A passed actual-motor witness and replay demonstrate one feasible action sequence under the contract, not mathematical feasibility of every rejected case or freedom from screening bias. The fixed initial configuration, one sphere, short 3–8 cm paths and four-second horizon restrict the scope. Supplementary scenes are generated after development within that known task family, after validation-only APF selection. Their success/failure records can strengthen comparison to the validation-tuned APF, but cannot become an untouched independent research study or be pooled with the original test as fresh independent evidence.

## Completed supplementary result

The separately frozen supplementary evaluation contains 100 newly accepted scenarios from 19 template groups: far 41, near-link 49, and tight 10. In order, tracking/fixed APF/tuned APF/PPO best 550901/551901/552901 achieve **93/99/100/98/98/99 complete successes out of 100**. All 13 failed episodes first enter the external contact band; no self-contact-band failure occurs in this new batch. The three policies therefore **do not outperform the validation-tuned APF** here. This leaves the original claim intact—higher success counts than fixed APF on the original test—but prevents extending it to tuned APF or the whole known task family.

Only 91 scenarios are complete successes for every one of the six primary methods; nonmissing external minimum-distance metrics use n=50. In the same order, mean command smoothness is 0.033835/0.132750/0.084545/0.413033/0.340939/0.130293 rad/s². Raw actor component clip percentages are 44.0795/55.1127/10.7315, while decision-any-clip percentages are 97.7507/98.0025/71.9265, for the three PPO policies respectively. These retain the success–motion-quality distinction on a common completed subset, not failure prefixes.

Tuned APF gains one scenario and loses none relative to fixed APF. That scenario, `me5418-supplement-v1-0074`, is a post hoc contrast: fixed APF fails at 0.716667 s with an external distance of 0.088445 mm, while tracking, tuned APF, and all three policies complete. On `me5418-supplement-v1-0073`, tracking and both APFs complete but seeds 550901 and 551901 fail at 0.704167 and 2.845833 s. Thus added PPO posture actions can damage a previously successful tracking trajectory on a new scene. Seed 552901 gains scene 0074 but loses scene 0001 relative to fixed APF, giving zero net change. Exact IDs, templates, categories and all failed prefixes remain indexed.

For PPO minus tuned APF, paired template-cluster percentile ranges in percentage points are: seed 550901 −2.000 [−5.634, 0.000], seed 551901 −2.000 [−7.317, 0.000], seed 552901 −1.000 [−3.659, 0.000]. Tuned minus fixed APF is +1.000 [0.000, 3.750]. These are descriptive ranges under the declared group resampling, not significance tests. There were 288 predeclared generation candidates, 136 geometry rejections, 52 candidates not run after the acceptance target was met, 101 actual-motor witness attempts and 100 successful replays. Witness sources are 99 fixed APF and one gentle APF, giving especially strong APF-family screening. The far fraction is 41%, compared with 36/131 in the original test. Rates across the two batches must not be combined or treated as evidence of before/after policy improvement.

APF validation search/generation/evaluation consume 424.689/204.068/512.630 s respectively, each within its own 1,800 s limit. The scientific supplementary workload is 1,323 episodes (522 search + 101 witness + 100 replay + 600 evaluation), with no formal training interactions. These scopes exclude unrelated code tests, environment installation, analysis, report rendering and packaging; they are not total project development cost.

## Actual engineering reflection and future work

The useful engineering lessons are concrete: preserve the frozen numerical core while adapting paths; distinguish executable feasibility witnesses from policy features; retain first failure and null endpoints; report each policy on the same IDs; inspect raw actor outputs before legal clipping; and rebuild dependencies in an independent environment before claiming portability. The assistant performed implementation and analysis support; the text does not assign unverified independent authorship.

A future learning study could separately evaluate self-geometry signals, reward scaling, smoother bounded actor outputs, exact SVD secondary projection, and initial-state variation. Each changes representation, objective, or execution and needs a new predeclared version, training budget, witness verification, and further held-out data. The supplementary APF comparison uses the existing frozen policies and does not justify additional training merely because a peer's policy performs differently.
